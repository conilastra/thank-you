What happens next

Five steps. You do very few of them.

  1. 1
    Now

    Confirm the invite

    Accept the calendar invite so the slot is held. Need to move it? Use the reschedule link in your confirmation email.

  2. 2
    The call · 30 minutes

    We check fit and plan the connections

    We talk through how you work, which tools you want connected, and what you want your brain to know. No pitch deck. If it isn't a fit, we'll tell you.

  3. 3
    After the call

    You get the intake

    One 60-minute intake conversation, plus a short list of the files and accounts you choose to share. That's the whole of your effort up front.

  4. 4
    7 days

    We collect, build and connect

    We gather what you've already said, written and decided, structure it, and list every place your own records contradict each other. The 7-day clock starts when we have your intake and files.

  5. 5
    Handover

    The proof test

    We ask 20 questions you already know the answers to. It has to answer them correctly, with sources. When it does, you pay the second half and it's yours. If it doesn't, we keep working at no cost.

Before the call · optional, 5 minutes

Two things that make the call count

List your tools

Jot down where your information lives today. Anything with an export, a connector or an API can feed your brain.

  • Meetings and recordings
  • Email and calendar (optional)
  • Documents and drives
  • Chat and voice notes

Pick 3 questions

Write down three questions you wish you could ask your business and get a straight answer to, for example:

  • "What did I promise, and to whom?"
  • "Why did we decide that?"

You don't need to gather or send any files yet. We sort that out together.

Our promises

What we will and won't do

You stay in control

Your files stay in your own Drive. Nothing is connected without your say-so, and email and calendar are entirely your call.

It never guesses

Every answer cites the file it came from. Unknowns stay marked as unknown. It drafts for your review and never posts or sends as you.

P.S. Not sure how to answer something before the call? Don't worry about it. That's what the call is for. Bring what you have.
